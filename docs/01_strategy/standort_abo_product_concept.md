# PixelRing Standort-Abo Product Concept

Status: internal product concept, not an approved public offer
Date: 2026-05-21
Owner: strategy documentation

## Purpose

This document captures the working concept for `PixelRing Standort-Abo` (подписка на контроль и обслуживание объектов) as a future B2B service direction for PixelRing.

The concept should guide later work on:

- B2B offer design;
- public website messaging;
- service-contract packaging;
- CRM and portal requirements;
- operations planning;
- sales discovery for multi-location clients.

This is not yet a confirmed pricing model, public tariff, legal promise, SLA, or implemented platform feature.

## Product Boundary

`PixelRing Standort-Abo` (подписка на контроль и обслуживание объектов) must not be sold as "unlimited repair by subscription".

The stronger positioning is:

**PixelRing manages the visible brand infrastructure of business locations.**

This includes signs, light advertising, window graphics, printed materials, menus, posters, navigation, facade elements, mounting condition, electrical risks, material freshness, cleanliness, and brand consistency across one or many locations.

The product must preserve the core PixelRing model:

- one accountable service company;
- one entry point for the customer;
- AI-assisted intake where useful;
- human specialists execute and coordinate real service work;
- no marketplace, contractor directory, listing platform, or "find a master" framing.

## Current Strategic Fit

PixelRing already has the basis for this direction:

- repair and maintenance of advertising installations;
- light advertising, LED, neon, power supplies, controllers, transformers, wiring, mounting, frames, cleaning, optical restoration, mounting, dismantling, and relocation;
- print and branding materials such as posters, banners, stickers, foils, menus, and branch materials;
- `Wartung & Serviceverträge` (обслуживание и сервисные договоры) as an approved concept on the `Leistungen` (услуги) page;
- up to 20% benefit on selected advertising materials when a maintenance or service contract exists, using the approved wording only;
- future product direction around structured service processes for businesses with repeated needs or multiple locations.

Safe strategic formula:

**PixelRing watches the visible customer-facing elements of a business location so they work, shine, stay current, remain safe, and match the brand.**

## Guardrails

### Public Claim Boundary

Until separately approved, do not present `PixelRing Standort-Abo` (подписка на контроль и обслуживание объектов) as:

- a finished subscription platform;
- a fixed public tariff system;
- unlimited repair for a flat monthly price;
- a guaranteed SLA;
- a full customer portal with audit reports, invoices, warranties, and object management;
- a legal safety certification;
- coverage for every region, object, storm damage, vandalism event, permit, lift, spare part, or full reconstruction.

### Safe Public Direction

Safe wording for early public use:

- `Wartung und laufende Betreuung können individuell besprochen werden.` (обслуживание и постоянное сопровождение можно обсудить индивидуально)
- `Für mehrere Standorte entsteht ein strukturierter Serviceprozess.` (для нескольких объектов создается структурированный сервисный процесс)
- `PixelRing prüft, dokumentiert und koordiniert den nächsten sinnvollen Schritt.` (PixelRing проверяет, документирует и координирует следующий разумный шаг)
- `Bis zu 20% Vorteil auf ausgewählte Werbematerialien bei bestehendem Wartungs- oder Servicevertrag.` (до 20% преимущества на выбранные рекламные материалы при действующем договоре обслуживания или сервисном договоре)

### Economic Boundary

The subscription should cover:

- audit;
- checks;
- documentation;
- reporting;
- coordination;
- preventive recommendations;
- small agreed maintenance tasks where explicitly included.

Repairs beyond the included scope should be separate, usually with:

- agreed rates;
- material discounts where approved;
- separate approval for spare parts;
- separate approval for lifts, height work, permits, storm damage, vandalism, full redesign, or large replacement structures.

## Core Customer Problem

Businesses with 3, 10, 50, or 200 locations often do not systematically know:

- whether signs are visible at night;
- whether foils are peeling from windows;
- whether posters, menus, or seasonal materials are outdated;
- whether facade elements are loose;
- whether electrical or moisture risks exist;
- whether the brand looks consistent across locations;
- which defects are urgent and which can be planned;
- who owns photos, checks, contractors, approvals, and reporting.

## PixelRing Solution

`PixelRing Standort-Abo` (подписка на контроль и обслуживание объектов) gives the client a structured service layer:

- location register;
- register of visible brand and advertising assets;
- recurring remote or on-site checks;
- photo documentation;
- problem detection before customer complaints;
- prioritized task list;
- coordinated repair and material updates;
- one responsible service point.

The client does not buy "repairs by subscription". The client buys control over visible brand infrastructure.

## Offer Architecture

### MVP Offer 1: PixelRing Check

Goal: affordable entry product for smaller chains and businesses that need recurring visibility control.

Includes:

- monthly remote check;
- photo checklist;
- short report per location;
- visibility, light, brand, and safety notes;
- task priority list;
- repair recommendations;
- optional route into a paid service request.

Positioning:

**Regular visibility control before defects become customer-visible.**

### MVP Offer 2: PixelRing Care

Goal: mid-tier service for restaurants, salons, clinics, and retail locations that need active appearance maintenance.

Includes:

- everything in `PixelRing Check` (регулярный контроль объектов);
- scheduled on-site visits, for example quarterly;
- small preventive maintenance where agreed;
- cleaning of accessible elements where agreed;
- replacement of simple print or branch materials where agreed;
- material and repair benefit where approved;
- recommendations for LED, power supplies, foils, and printed materials.

Positioning:

**The location should keep looking serviceable, current, and brand-consistent, not only be repaired after failure.**

### MVP Offer 3: PixelRing Network

Goal: B2B offer for 10+ locations, franchises, facility management, and central marketing or operations teams.

Includes:

- onboarding audit across locations;
- asset register;
- location-level status overview;
- brand consistency checks;
- risk and urgency classification;
- centralized service requests;
- annual repair and update budget planning;
- optional priority handling for urgent defects.

Positioning:

**One service structure instead of many disconnected local repair paths.**

## Extended Product Variants

These variants are useful for later packaging, but should not be treated as final tariffs.

### PixelRing Audit Basic

One-time entry audit for clients who first need to understand the state of their locations.

Includes:

- initial location inspection;
- photo documentation of advertising and brand elements;
- list of signs, light elements, foils, posters, menus, and navigation;
- condition assessment;
- safety, electrical, mounting, and appearance risks;
- repair and budget recommendations.

Sales line:

`In 1-2 Wochen erhalten Sie eine Zustandskarte Ihrer sichtbaren Markenelemente je Standort.` (за 1-2 недели вы получаете карту состояния видимых бренд-элементов по каждому объекту)

Commercial note: can be sold as a one-time audit and partially credited into a 12-month service agreement if approved.

### PixelRing Monthly Check-up

Recurring monthly check focused on visibility and practical task prioritization.

Includes:

- sign visibility check;
- before/after photos where work happens;
- evening light check where available;
- damage, dirt, fading, peeling, and outdated-material control;
- menu, poster, and promo-material review;
- short report per location;
- task classification: urgent, planned, observe.

Check format:

- remote photo checklist from branch staff or manager;
- PixelRing analysis and task creation;
- quarterly or semiannual on-site visit for selected locations.

### PixelRing Visibility Care

Appearance-focused maintenance product.

Includes:

- recurring cleaning of accessible elements;
- minor repair where agreed;
- simple print-material replacement;
- sticker and poster updates;
- light element review;
- recommendations for LED, power supplies, and foils;
- selected material benefit where approved.

### PixelRing Light & Sign Maintenance

Technical maintenance product for illuminated signs, LED, neon, lightboxes, and facade letters.

Includes:

- quarterly or semiannual technical inspection;
- power supply, LED module, controller, cable, transformer, and mounting checks;
- moisture, corrosion, and overheating diagnostics;
- night light check;
- risk report;
- priority repair path where approved.

### PixelRing Safety & Compliance Check

Risk-reduction product for owners, facility managers, and chains.

Includes:

- mounting check;
- stability observations;
- photo documentation of potentially risky elements;
- open cable, moisture, corrosion, and damage review;
- recommendation for VDE/DIN review where appropriate;
- digital protocol where implemented;
- priority handling of dangerous cases where approved.

Claim boundary: this should be sold as documented preventive inspection and operational risk reduction, not as a legal guarantee.

### PixelRing Brand Consistency Subscription

Brand-control product for chains and franchises.

Includes:

- unified appearance standards;
- branch checks using a brand checklist;
- color, menu, poster, window-sticker, and sign consistency review;
- outdated-material control;
- advertising-material updates;
- monthly branch score;
- report for marketing and operations teams.

### PixelRing Emergency SLA Add-on

Possible paid add-on for clients with recurring contracts.

Includes:

- priority reaction path;
- dedicated channel where approved;
- emergency classification;
- urgent securing of dangerous elements;
- faster dispatch for critical cases such as dark sign, loose sign, storm damage, open cable, or pedestrian risk.

Claim boundary: no public SLA promise until operational capacity, geography, response windows, and exclusions are defined.

## Operating Process

### Step 1: Onboarding Audit

PixelRing collects or creates the first location card:

- address;
- business type;
- on-site contact;
- list of signs and visible brand elements;
- material types;
- day and night photos where relevant;
- condition;
- risk level;
- warranty context where known;
- past repairs where known;
- priorities.

### Step 2: Asset Register

Each object should eventually have a digital asset map.

Example:

`Berlin Mitte - facade sign, 3D letters, LED, condition 78/100, last check 2026-05-12, risk: medium, task: replace 2 LED modules and update window sticker.` (Berlin Mitte - фасадная вывеска, объемные буквы, LED, состояние 78/100, последняя проверка 12.05.2026, риск: средний, задача: заменить 2 LED-модуля и обновить витринную наклейку)

Strategic value:

**The client buys control over assets that were previously unmanaged.**

### Step 3: Monthly Health Score

Each location can receive simple scores:

- `Visibility Score` (оценка видимости): whether the location is visible and readable;
- `Light Score` (оценка подсветки): whether illuminated elements work;
- `Brand Score` (оценка бренд-соответствия): whether materials match the current brand and campaign;
- `Safety Score` (оценка безопасности): whether visible risks exist;
- `Urgency Level` (уровень срочности): urgent, planned, observe.

### Step 4: Planned Checks

Possible rhythm:

- monthly: remote photo checklist;
- quarterly: on-site visit for higher-risk or higher-value objects;
- semiannually: technical inspection of light elements;
- annually: full audit report and next-year budget plan.

### Step 5: Repair And Updates

When a defect is found, PixelRing creates a service task:

- what is damaged;
- photos;
- risk;
- estimated cost where possible;
- timeline;
- approval owner;
- status;
- warranty context;
- object history.

`30-Tage Standort-Audit` (30-дневный аудит объектов)

Offer:

**PixelRing checks your locations, creates a photo register of visible advertising elements, and shows where visibility, brand quality, and safety are being lost.**

After the audit, PixelRing can show:

## Commercial Logic

The subscription solves four expensive business problems:

1. Loss of control  
   "We do not know what is happening at our locations."

2. Loss of brand quality  
   "Branches look different, materials are outdated, signs are dark."

3. Unexpected cost  
   "Every repair is urgent, chaotic, and expensive."

4. Safety risk  
   "Mounting, electricity, moisture, and facade elements are not checked systematically."
