## Article 1: Leuchtstoffröhren in der Werbeanlage ersetzen oder auf LED umrüsten?

Proposed public slug:

```text
leuchtstoffroehren-werbeanlage-ersetzen-led-umruesten
```

### Why This Is A Real Problem

The customer sees an older `Leuchtkasten` (световой короб) or illuminated sign that still partly works, but maintenance is becoming unclear: old `Leuchtstoffröhren` (люминесцентные трубки) fail again, light is dim or uneven, replacement tubes may not match, and the owner does not know whether to keep replacing tubes or move toward `LED-Modernisierung` (LED-модернизация).

This is not only a search topic. It is a real operational decision: keep the old lighting setup, partially modernize it, rebuild the inner LED layout, or consider a replacement solution.

### Primary Customer Situation

- the sign still partly works but looks weak, patchy, or inconsistent;
- old tubes are replaced repeatedly;
- different tubes create different color or brightness;
- maintenance effort grows;
- the customer wants to avoid unnecessary full replacement.

### Must Not Duplicate

- `Werbeanlage flackert` (вывеска мерцает) as instability;
- `LED leuchtet ungleichmäßig` (LED светит неравномерно) as a symptom;
- `Buchstabe leuchtet nicht` (буква не светится) as local outage;
- `Werbeanlage leuchtet nicht` (вывеска не светится) as total outage;
- the service page `/leistungen/lichtwerbung-led-modernisierung` as a commercial offer.

### Small Card Angle

```text
Alte Röhren im Leuchtkasten wieder defekt: ersetzen oder LED-Modernisierung prüfen?
```

### Full Article Outline

- how the customer recognizes a problem with old tubes, `Vorschaltgerät` (пускорегулирующий блок), or old lighting layout;
- typical symptoms: dim light, delayed start, mismatched colors, repeated tube replacement, dark zones;
- why simply replacing tubes does not always solve the underlying problem;
- when `Reparatur` (ремонт) can still make sense;
- when `LED-Umrüstung` (переход на LED) can be more practical;
- what can be checked safely from outside without opening the sign;
- what not to do: no housing opening, no wiring, no `Vorschaltgerät` or power work, no unqualified tube conversion;
- what PixelRing checks: housing, access, moisture, power, lighting layout, surface, required brightness;
- possible paths: targeted replacement, partial modernization, new LED inner layout, replacement solution;
- what to send: day/evening photos, short switch-on video, dimensions, access, address, approximate age.

### Internal Links

- `/leistungen/lichtwerbung-led-modernisierung`
- `/leistungen/werbeanlagen-reparatur`
- `/leistungen/werbeanlagen-audit-diagnose`
- `/probleme-loesungen/werbeanlage-flackert`
- `/probleme-loesungen/led-leuchtet-ungleichmaessig`
- `/probleme-loesungen/werbeanlage-leuchtet-nicht`

### Safety And Claim Boundaries

- do not teach opening the housing;
- do not teach wiring, `Vorschaltgerät` replacement, `Netzteil` replacement, terminal work, or LED-module replacement;
- do not promise a fixed energy-saving percentage;
- do not promise payback period;
- do not claim LED is always better;
- frame LED as a checked option after diagnosis.

### Priority And Dependencies

Priority: high after the LED service page modernization.

Dependency: `/leistungen/lichtwerbung-led-modernisierung` should be strengthened first, so the future article can link to a mature service page.

## Article 2: Neonreklame reparieren oder auf LED umrüsten?

Proposed public slug:

```text
neonreklame-reparieren-led-umruesten
```

### Why This Is A Real Problem

The customer has an existing `Neonreklame` (неоновая реклама) that is important for the object identity, but parts are dark, unstable, noisy, old, or difficult to maintain. The practical question is whether to preserve the `Neon-Charakter` (неоновый характер), repair the defective part, partially modernize, or check a LED alternative.

This is a real brand and technical decision, not just SEO content.

### Primary Customer Situation

- part of the neon sign is dark or unreliable;
- neon tubes, transformer, mounting, or moisture are suspected;
- the sign is important for the place's visual identity;
- the customer wants lower maintenance but does not want to lose the look;
- safety and access are unclear.

### Must Not Duplicate

- `Buchstabe leuchtet nicht` (буква не светится) as local outage;
- `Werbeanlage flackert` (вывеска мерцает) as instability;
- `Werbeanlage schaltet nach Regen ab` (вывеска отключается после дождя) as moisture outage;
- general LED modernization service-page copy.

### Small Card Angle

```text
Neon teilweise dunkel oder unzuverlässig: reparieren oder LED-Modernisierung prüfen?
```

### Full Article Outline

- neon symptoms requiring review: dark sections, flicker, weak glow, noise, smell, visible moisture;
- likely directions: tube, `Trafo` (трансформатор), connections, moisture, housing age, access;
- what can be safely checked from outside: photos, video, visible water/cracks/sagging, when the fault appears;
- when neon repair can make sense: design preservation, isolated defect, available components, safe housing;
- when `LED-Umrüstung` (переход на LED) can be more reasonable: repeated failures, difficult access, outdated system, maintenance requirements;
- what PixelRing checks: technical condition, visual effect, housing, access, safety, options for preserving the look;
- possible paths: local repair, component replacement, partial modernization, `LED-Neonlook` (LED в стиле неона), new light element;
- what to send: facade overview, close-ups of affected letters/lines, switch-on video, safe photos of visible accessible components, dimensions, address;
- urgent signs: crackling, smell, sparks, water inside, breaker trips, damaged mounting.

### Internal Links

- `/leistungen/lichtwerbung-led-modernisierung`
- `/leistungen/werbeanlagen-reparatur`
- `/leistungen/werbeanlagen-audit-diagnose`
- `/leistungen/montage-demontage-werbeanlagen`
- `/probleme-loesungen/buchstabe-leuchtet-nicht`
- `/probleme-loesungen/werbeanlage-flackert`
- `/probleme-loesungen/werbeanlage-schaltet-nach-regen-ab`

### Safety And Claim Boundaries

- do not explain work with `Hochspannung` (высокое напряжение);
- do not teach transformer work, wiring, soldering, tube handling, or height access;
- do not promise full preservation of the look before review;
- do not claim PixelRing produces `Neonröhren` (неоновые трубки) unless that scope is explicitly confirmed;
- do not say neon must always be replaced by LED.

### Priority And Dependencies

Priority: medium-high after the first fluorescent-tube article.

Dependency: write after the LED service page is modernized and after the first article is approved, to avoid mixing old lightbox tube decisions with neon-specific decisions.
