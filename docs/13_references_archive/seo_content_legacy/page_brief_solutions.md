# Page Brief — Solutions

Approved implementation plan:

- `docs/02_public_website/page_plan_solutions.md`

## Purpose

`Probleme & Loesungen` is the problem-first page for visitors who think in outcomes and visible failures, not in service terminology.

## Goal

- capture visitors who do not know the right service category;
- translate symptoms into a structured request path;
- route urgent and unclear cases into the same controlled intake flow.
- support SEO/GEO visibility for common sign, light advertising, lettering, film, and storefront-branding problems without becoming a generic FAQ page.

## Approved Naming And Route

- DE menu label: `Probleme & Loesungen`
- EN menu label: `Problems & Solutions`
- RU menu label: `Проблемы и решения`
- TR menu label: `Sorunlar ve Cozumler`
- PL menu label: `Problemy i rozwiazania`
- AR menu label: `المشكلات والحلول`
- Canonical route: `/de/probleme-loesungen`
- Localized route pattern: `/[locale]/probleme-loesungen`

`Support Center` may remain a supporting knowledge/help concept, but it is not the main navigation label for this page.

`FAQ` is acceptable as a section on the page, not as the page identity.

## What Must Be On The Page

- short hero explaining that visitors can start from the problem, not the technical term;
- grouped scenarios such as:
  - sign does not light up;
  - flickering or unstable light;
  - weather or water damage;
  - damaged letters or box elements;
  - urgent dismantling or safety risk;
  - restoration or refresh after wear;
- short guidance on what information or photos help;
- an animated before/after impact panel showing cautious operational/visibility improvements such as visibility, site impression, customer orientation, and reduced outage risk;
- CTA to submit a request;
- optional route to support knowledge where it helps the user self-classify.

## What Must Be Avoided

- deep troubleshooting that replaces service intake;
- advice that creates liability or unsafe self-repair behavior;
- support-center sprawl in the main conversion path.
- guaranteed uplift claims or exact performance percentages without documented proof.
- changing existing support articles during the first page implementation step.

## SEO Direction

- SEO title: `Probleme mit Werbeanlagen? Typische Schaeden & Loesungen | PixelRing`
- H1: `Typische Probleme mit Werbeanlagen erkennen und richtig loesen`
- FAQ/search content should sit below the primary problem-to-solution interaction.
- Existing `/support` and `/support/[slug]` articles remain intact until the final article/content optimization step.

## MVP Role In Navigation

This page answers: `I know the problem, but what exactly should I request?`
