# Page Brief — Services

## Purpose

`Услуги` is the scope page for what PixelRing actually does as one accountable service company.

It must not read like a marketplace catalog or a manufacturer mega-menu.

## Goal

- show the service scope quickly;
- reduce uncertainty about whether the company handles the request;
- route the visitor into `Подать заявку`.

## What Must Be On The Page

- short hero explaining the service scope in plain language;
- clear service groups:
  - sign repair;
  - installation and dismantling;
  - illuminated advertising and LED;
  - branding and film application;
  - maintenance and recovery;
- short notes on what happens after request intake;
- strong CTA to submit a request;
- optional proof strip with examples or trust markers.

## What Must Be Avoided

- giant product taxonomy;
- marketplace wording;
- over-promising manufacturing breadth that is not operationally confirmed;
- dead-end lists without a CTA.

## MVP Role In Navigation

This page answers: `Do you handle my type of work?`
