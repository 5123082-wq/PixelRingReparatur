# Page Brief — For Business

## Purpose

`Для бизнеса` is the B2B trust and process page for commercial clients with more coordination needs than a simple one-off request.

## Goal

- show that PixelRing can work in a controlled business process;
- support network clients, property managers, agencies, and multi-location operations;
- convert larger accounts without making the whole site feel enterprise-heavy.

## What Must Be On The Page

- short hero for commercial and multi-location service scenarios;
- target groups:
  - single-location business owners;
  - chains and multi-site operations;
  - agencies and intermediaries;
  - commercial landlords or facility managers;
- process blocks:
  - one accountable contact;
  - standardized intake;
  - status visibility;
  - coordinated execution;
  - optional maintenance/service continuity;
- trust layer around responsibility, communication, and operational control;
- CTA to submit a business request.

## What Must Be Avoided

- fake enterprise claims;
- procurement language that the product cannot yet support;
- implying a full client portal or RBAC platform that is not implemented.

## MVP Role In Navigation

This page answers: `Can you handle business complexity without chaos?`
